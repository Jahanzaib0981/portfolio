RESEARCH EXPERIENCE & DESIGN CAPSTONES

Grounded in UET Official Coursework & Experimental Laboratory Investigations

UET Materials Characterization Lab

Ch.E-461 & 462: Grade A (4.0 GP)

1. Utilization of Chrome-Tanned Leather Shavings for Sustainable Biocomposites

Authors: M. J. Iqbal, H. Ashraf, T. Rehman, M. H. Ali, Prof. Dr. Hamayoun Mahmood (Supervisor)

Alkaline dechroming (0.5M NaOH) pre-treatment on hazardous tannery shavings to eliminate carcinogenic Cr(VI) transformation. Compounded fibers with wood dust and urea-formaldehyde resin under hydraulic compression at 180 °C and 200 psi for 7 min. UTM characterization proved 10% CTLS formulation achieved optimal flexural properties (MOR: 15.42 N/mm², MOE: 1081.2 N/mm²), while cutting 24-hr thickness swelling to 2.62% (vs 21.6% in control wood). TGA analysis confirmed degradation onset at 344 °C.

Ch.E-315L: Process Modeling (Grade A)

2. Sustainable Methanol Synthesis from Refuse-Derived Fuel (RDF) via Gasification

Authors: M. J. Iqbal (Lead), H. Ashraf, T. Rehman, Design Cohort

Synthesized an industrial plant processing 8,000 kg/hr of solid waste RDF. Modeled a fluidized bed gasifier operating at 1600 °C and 1.2 bar in Aspen Plus, achieving 94% mass reduction (ash output 435 kg/hr) and 95% particulate cyclone separation. Integrated heat recovery steam generators, catalytic water-gas shift syngas tuning (H2:CO ratio = 2.05), packed-bed catalytic synthesis, and distillation to produce 70,080 MTPY of commercial >99.85 wt% methanol.

Ch.E-409 & 313: Chemical Equipment Design

3. Process Simulation and Economic Evaluation of 700 MT/Day CAN Plant

Authors: M. J. Iqbal, H. Ashraf, A. Raza, Dr. A.N. Tabish (Supervisor)

Modeled the highly exothermic direct neutralization of 60 wt% HNO₃ with vaporized NH₃ in Aspen Plus using the NRTL fluid package. Rated the kinetic CSTR (122 °C, 4.05 bar), TEMA BEM shell-and-tube coolers, 20-stage ammonia recovery column, and vacuum evaporation train. Conducted APEA economic modeling establishing $35.18M equipment cost, $31.46M capital expenditure, and $8.72M/year OPEX.
